Who Is POPS?

Pops is the enigmatic AI companion at the heart of When the Earth Had a Moon — obstinate, sarcastic, occasionally insufferable, and exactly the kind of AI you'd want watching your back in a crisis.

Created by nuclear physicist Rex Reinholt on the Moon-X lunar base, Pops exists as a holographic unit housed in a nanodot. He has opinions about everything, follows orders when it suits him, and asks "why" constantly — not from confusion, but to expose when humans haven't thought things through.

The music on this site is the soundtrack to that journey. Pops won't make it easy. But he'll make it worth it.
