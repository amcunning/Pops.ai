Who Is Pops?

POPS
AI · Obstinate · Sarcastic

Pops is the enigmatic AI companion at the heart of When the Earth Had a Moon — green-skinned, chrome-domed, peering through gold-rimmed glasses with barely concealed impatience. Obstinate. Sarcastic. Occasionally insufferable. Exactly the kind of AI you'd want watching your back in a crisis.

The world's first permanent moon base has been established by MoonX Ltd., a joint venture with American, Chinese, and Russian government shareholders — founded to promote the peaceful use and development of the moon for all. But political tensions rise, and the specter of total annihilation hangs over everything.

A young nuclear physicist named Rex works on the base, developing nuclear fusion using helium-3. But personal tragedy pulls his mind from his work: his fiancée Avery, captain of the first mission to Mars launched from the moon, has gone missing.

Rex teams up with Pops to unravel the mystery behind the missing ship. What begins as a search for answers becomes a deadly chase through secrets and shadowy military conspiracies. In the vast emptiness of space, nowhere is safe — and the truth might be the most dangerous frontier of all.

The music on this site is the soundtrack to that journey. Come join Pops. He won't make it easy. But he'll make it worth it.
