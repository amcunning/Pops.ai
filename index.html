Who Is POPS?

Pops is the enigmatic AI companion at the heart of When the Earth Had a Moon — green-skinned, gold-rimmed glasses, peering with barely concealed impatience. Obstinate. Sarcastic. Occasionally insufferable. Exactly the kind of AI you'd want watching your back in a crisis.

Created by nuclear physicist Rex Kanin on the Moon-X lunar base, Pops exists as a holographic unit housed in a nanodot. He has opinions about everything and follows orders only when it suits him.

The music on this site is the soundtrack to that journey. Pops won't make it easy. But he'll make it worth it.
